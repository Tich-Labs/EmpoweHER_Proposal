The Challenge

Physical cards solve yesterday's
distribution problem.

The RFP calls for a card game. We will deliver one — and then some. Because the research already tells us where the gaps are, and they're not in the format. They're in reach.

The assumption

AGYW will gather around a facilitator's card table

Physical card games are powerful in controlled safe-space settings — but they sit on a shelf between sessions.

"I haven't seen billboards. I haven't really seen posters anywhere outside of the hospital."

— AGYW, Nairobi, Kenya · MOSAIC 2023
The research insight

The only PrEP information she found was on TikTok

MOSAIC's 2023 validation with 121 AGYW across Kenya, South Africa, and Zimbabwe found that young women want PrEP information in places they go every day.

"Someone was talking about it in a video on TikTok. From the comments it was clear that lots of people don't know what it was."

— AGYW, Zimbabwe · MOSAIC 2023

Our Approach

We don't challenge the card game format — we expand it. Every card can be shared on WhatsApp, embedded in a peer educator's phone, or played when no facilitator is present.
